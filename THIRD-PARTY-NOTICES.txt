=== Test tools (not included) ===
The instruction tests (tests/epicycle/lab.py, tests/altivec-compare.py) use
Unicorn and Capstone as external Python packages; they are not distributed
or linked. G4 opcode metadata in the tests was cross-checked against
Motorola/Freescale/NXP ALTIVECPEM Rev. 3 and MPC7450UM Rev. 5 instruction
tables; it contains instruction names and encodings, not the manuals' text.
